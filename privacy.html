Privacy Policy for Tend

Last updated 11 September 2026.

Tend is a task tracker where finishing things grows a garden. This page says exactly what it stores, where, and who else can ever see it. It is written to be read rather than to cover anybody, so if something here is unclear, treat that as a fault worth reporting.

The short version. In local mode nothing you type ever leaves your device. In cloud mode your tasks and garden are stored in a private database so they can follow you between devices, and only your display name and the picture of your garden are visible to other signed-in people. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

The two modes

Local mode (the default)

No sign-in and no account. Everything — tasks, categories, notes, coins, the whole garden — is written to your browser's own storage on that one device. None of it is transmitted anywhere. Nobody, including the operator of this site, can see it. Clearing your browser data deletes it permanently, and the only copy is whatever you have exported yourself.

Cloud mode

You create an account with an email address and a password so your tasks follow you between devices. The data lives in a Supabase Postgres database. Row-level security is switched on for every table and every policy checks your own user id, so one account cannot read another's rows.

A copy of all of it is also kept in your browser so the app keeps working offline, and is pushed up when you are back on a connection.

What other people can see: the Friends tab

This is the one place your data is visible to anyone else, so it is worth being precise. Signed-in users of this site can see, for every other account:

  • the display name
  • which world was chosen, and the gardener's appearance
  • the garden itself — its plants, land, trees, tools, saplings, cabins and pets

They cannot see your tasks, your notes, your categories, your email address or your password. Those columns are not part of the shared view at all, so there is no setting that could leak them by accident. If you would rather not appear at all, use local mode — an account with no garden does not appear in the list.

That is the complete list. Tend contains no analytics, no advertising network, no tracking pixels, no social media SDKs, and no cookies beyond the single sign-in token that keeps you logged in.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account exists. There is no separate archive, no backup that outlives a deletion, and nothing is retained for analysis after you go.

Deleting your data

Both of these live in Settings → Danger zone inside the app:

  • Erase all my data — empties the account: every task, category and garden change. The sign-in itself stays, so you can start again.
  • Delete my account — removes the account itself. The sign-in, the tasks, the categories, the garden and your entry in Friends are deleted from the server immediately and cannot be recovered. There is no waiting period and no need to ask anybody.

Export backup, in the same Settings panel, writes everything to a .json file you keep. Do that first if there is any chance you want it back.

Children

Tend is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information. It asks for nothing beyond an email address in cloud mode.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete your data. Tend is built so you can do all four yourself, immediately, from the Settings panel, without asking permission. If you would rather have help with it, use the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

If what Tend stores ever changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. Material changes will also appear in What's new inside the app.

Running your own copy

Tend is open source and anyone can host it. This policy describes this installation. If you are using a copy someone else has deployed, their database is the one holding your data and you should ask them.
